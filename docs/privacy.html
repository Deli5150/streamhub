Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

StreamHub is an app that runs on your own devices (Mac, Windows, Linux, Steam Deck, iPhone and Android). It has no servers of its own, no user accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. This policy explains what the app does with information, and where that information goes.

Information that stays on your device

  • Platform sign-ins. You sign in to Twitch, Kick and Rumble on those platforms' own sign-in pages, shown inside the app. The sign-in cookies they set are kept on your device, the same way a web browser keeps them. StreamHub uses them only to show the channels you follow, who's live, their videos and chat.
  • Google sign-in tokens. When you sign in with Google, the access tokens Google issues are stored on your device, encrypted with your system's secure storage where it's available (for example the macOS Keychain or Windows' data protection).
  • Your settings and lists. Followed channels, settings, Watch Later, the queue, imported playlists and where you stopped watching a video are stored on your device.

Google user data

Signing in with Google is optional. StreamHub asks for two permissions, and uses each only for the feature you turn on:

  • YouTube, read-only (youtube.readonly): to read the channels you subscribe to, check which of them are live, show their videos, and list your playlists so you can import the ones you choose. StreamHub never posts, changes or deletes anything on your YouTube account.
  • Google Drive app data (drive.appdata), for Sync with Google: to keep one file in a hidden folder of your Google Drive that only StreamHub can access. It holds your settings, followed channels, combined channels, muted alerts, Watch Later, imported playlists and, so you don't have to sign in again on each device, your Twitch, Kick and Rumble sign-ins. StreamHub can't see any of your other Drive files.

This information goes only between your devices and Google. It isn't sent to the developer or to anyone else, isn't sold, isn't used for advertising, and isn't used to train any AI or machine-learning models. No person reads it.

StreamHub's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Connections the app makes

  • The platforms you use (Twitch, YouTube, Kick, Rumble), to show streams, videos and chat. They see your requests as they would from a web browser, under their own privacy policies.
  • Google, for sign-in, YouTube and Sync, when you use those features.
  • GitHub, to check for app updates and download them. GitHub sees the request, as it would for any visit.
  • Casting (Android): the stream's link is sent to the TV you pick on your network.

Feedback and bug reports

The Feedback button opens a form on GitHub in your browser. Nothing is sent until you submit it there, under your own GitHub account, and the report is public on GitHub. A bug report can include the app's recent log if you leave that option on; sign-in tokens, email addresses and your computer's user name are removed from it first, and you can read and edit it before submitting.

Your choices

  • Sign out of any platform in the app's Accounts screen; its sign-in is deleted from the device.
  • Turn off Sync with Google in the app. To delete the synced file as well, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → StreamHub → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove StreamHub's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything it stored on that device.

Children

StreamHub isn't directed at children under 13, and the platforms it connects to set their own minimum ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
