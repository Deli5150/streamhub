StreamHub

Everyone you follow on Twitch, YouTube, Kick and Rumble, live in one app.

StreamHub is a free app for Mac, Windows, Linux, Steam Deck, iPhone and Android. Sign in to each platform once, and it shows who's live across all of them. Watch streams and past videos, chat, save videos for later, and line them up in a queue. A Big Picture mode makes it work like a TV app with a game controller.

Sign in with Google is used for two things, both optional:

  • YouTube (read-only): to list the channels you subscribe to, see which are live, and import your playlists.
  • Sync with Google: to keep your settings, followed channels and Watch Later the same on all your devices, in a private app folder in your own Google Drive.

StreamHub has no servers of its own and doesn't collect anything about you. See the privacy policy for details.
